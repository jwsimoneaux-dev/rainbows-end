Getting started

What does Checklists do?

It gives you the checklists a skipper runs, worked out from your boat: pre-departure, engine start, casting off and docking, the passenger and crew briefings, the fuel dock, arrival, leaving the boat, returning to the boat, hurricane prep, the documents aboard, and getting ready for the Coast Guard Auxiliary’s Vessel Safety Check. It keeps a record of each list you finish, and the emergency cards are always one tap away. It works offline.

How do I set up my boat?

On the Boat tab, give the boat a name and answer the questions: sail, power or paddle; the fuel; the engines; her length; her registration and waters; and what is aboard (heads, a generator, a propane stove, shore power, a dinghy with an outboard). Tap Save the boat. Every list is built from these answers, so change them any time and the lists follow.

Why doesn’t my list show a line I expected?

Because it doesn’t apply to your boat as she is set up. A diesel never sees the gasoline lines, and a boat without a generator never sees its checks. Check her answers on the Boat tab. To add a line of your own, change the list (below).

Can I keep more than one boat?

Yes. On the Boat tab, tap Add another boat. The lists follow the boat you choose there, and the Records tab shows her records or every boat’s.

Running a list

How do I run a list?

Open it and tap Start. Some lists ask one thing first: the pre-departure asks the persons aboard, and hurricane prep asks where the boat will ride it out. Tick each line as you do it. Tap Finish when you are done.

What if a line doesn’t apply today?

Tap Skip beside it and, if you like, say why. The record shows it as skipped, with your reason.

I left a list half done. Can I come back to it?

Yes. Open the list and tap Continue. If it was started long ago, the app asks whether to keep going or start fresh; Start fresh asks twice before it clears the ticks.

Can I finish with lines not checked?

Yes. The record names each line that was skipped or not checked, so whoever reads it can see.

Records

Where are my finished lists?

On the Records tab. Each record shows the boat, the date and time, who completed it, and every line: done, skipped with its reason, or not checked.

How do I share a record?

Open it and tap Share as PDF or Share as text. The system’s share sheet sends it where you choose: Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files or a printer.

How do I delete a record?

Open it and tap Delete this record, then tap again to confirm.

Changing a list

Can I change a list for my boat?

Yes. Open the list and tap Change this list for her. You can hide a line, reword it, add a line of your own, and move lines up or down. Restore the whole list puts it back as it came.

Can I make a list of my own?

Yes. On the Checklists tab, tap New list. Start from nothing, or from a copy of one of the built-in lists.

The emergency cards

What are the emergency cards?

Numbered steps in large type, to read aloud: the Mayday call, the Pan-Pan call, man overboard, fire, flooding, abandon ship, collision, grounding, medical, carbon monoxide, lightning and engine failure. With an MMSI on the Boat tab, there is also a card for a distress alert sent by mistake. Each card names its source.

How do I get to them fast?

Three ways: the Emergency strip at the foot of every screen; pressing and holding the app’s icon on the Home Screen, then Mayday call or Emergency cards; or the Checklists widget on your Lock Screen.

What does the Mayday card fill in?

The boat’s name, her call sign or registration number if you entered one, her description, the persons aboard from your latest pre-departure (change the number on the card if it is different), and your position from the phone’s GPS once you allow it. With an MMSI, it starts with the radio’s red DISTRESS button.

Why does the card ask me to tap Use the GPS?

So the location question appears when you choose, not over the script the first time you need it. Tap Use the GPS once and allow it, and from then on the card reads the position each time it opens. If you said no, the card says so and offers Open Settings; until you turn it on, read your position from the chart plotter.

Does the app send the Mayday?

No. The card is the script you read into your VHF, and the app sends nothing. Follow your training and the instructions of the Coast Guard.

Can I change a card?

Yes, except the Mayday call, the Pan-Pan call and the false-alert card, which keep the Coast Guard’s order and words. Open a card and tap Change this card. A step you changed says EDITED, and Show the original shows the source’s words.

Is there a night view?

Yes. Switch on Night view at the foot of the Emergency tab. The whole app turns red on black.

The Vessel Safety Check

What is the Vessel Safety Check list for?

Getting your boat ready for a free check by a Coast Guard Auxiliary examiner, about 15 to 30 minutes at the dock. The list follows the examiner’s form and the federal requirements for her size and type. The examiner decides; the list only gets her ready.

How do I ask for a check?

The last line of the list, Find an examiner and request a check, opens cgaux.org/vsc.

Our other apps

Why do some lines open another app?

Where one of our apps goes deeper, the line points to it: the float plan, the provisions, the anchor, the fuel cost, the day’s log. If you have the app, the line opens it; if not, its App Store page opens inside Checklists.

I have Manifest. Which app should I use for the pre-departure and the passenger briefing?

Manifest, if you keep a log there: it records them with the day’s entry. For those two lists Checklists offers Open Manifest, or Use this list anyway to run them here.

Where do I keep expiration dates and maintenance?

In Rainbow’s End Manifest. Checklists keeps no maintenance, expiration dates or inventory.

What it is and is not

Does it work without a signal?

Yes, all of it except the links that open a web page or another app’s App Store page. The GPS needs no signal.

Does it work on the iPad?

Yes. Turned on its side, the list and the run sit side by side.

Is it a substitute for training?

No. Rainbow’s End Checklists is an aid to preparation, not a substitute for the skipper’s judgment.
