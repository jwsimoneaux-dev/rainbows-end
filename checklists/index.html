Rainbow’s End Checklists

Checklists built from your boat. Mayday one tap away.

Lines singled up, guests aboard, the forecast checked: what you forget is what was never on a list. Checklists works out your lists from the boat herself, keeps a record of each one you finish, and keeps the Mayday call one tap away. On your device, bought once, with no account.

In beta on TestFlight · headed for the App Store

Why it stands out

  • Worked out from your boatA generic list leaves you to skip what doesn’t apply. Here you tell the app about your boat once, and every list is built from that: a diesel never sees a gasoline line, a single-engine boat never sees “Port engine”, a boat without a generator never sees its checks.
  • A record with a name on itEach finished list is kept with the boat, the date and time, the name of whoever checked it, and every line done, skipped with its reason, or not checked. Share it as a PDF or as text.
  • Every list leads somewhereWhere one of our other apps goes deeper, the line points to it: the float plan, the provisions, the anchor, the fuel cost, the day’s log. If you have the app, the line opens it; if not, its App Store page opens inside Checklists.

What it does

  • Twelve listsPre-departure, engine start, casting off and docking, the passenger and crew briefings, the fuel dock, arrival, leaving the boat, returning to the boat, hurricane prep, the documents aboard, and getting ready for the Coast Guard Auxiliary’s Vessel Safety Check.
  • Every line is something to do“Check the oil on the dipstick.” “Show where the fire extinguishers are.” Each list names its sources.
  • Yours to changeHide a line, reword it, add your own, or put the lines in your own order, and restore the original whenever you like. Make lists of your own, or start from a copy of one of ours.
  • Run it, tick it, finish itTick each line or skip it with a note. When you finish, the app asks who completed it and keeps the record.
  • The emergency cardsMayday, Pan-Pan, man overboard, fire, flooding, abandon ship, collision, grounding, medical, carbon monoxide, lightning and engine failure, as numbered steps in large type to read aloud. Each names its source.
  • The Mayday call, filled inYour boat’s name, her description, the persons aboard from your last pre-departure, and your position from the phone’s GPS if you allow it.
  • Always in reachA strip on every screen, two actions on the app’s Home Screen icon, and a Lock Screen widget. The night view turns the cards red on black.
  • On the iPadThe list and the run side by side.
  • Entirely offlineNo account, no server of ours, no tracking. Nothing you enter leaves your device unless you share a record yourself. The position is read only for the Mayday card, and never stored or sent.

We built it aboard our ketch Rainbow, for the skipper who wants the boat run by the list, not by memory.

What it won’t do

  • Call for help. The Mayday card is the script you read into your VHF; the app sends nothing.
  • Pass your boat. The Vessel Safety Check list gets her ready for the examiner, and the examiner decides.
  • Keep maintenance, expiration dates or an inventory. Those are Rainbow’s End Manifest’s, the book of your boat.
  • Put your lists or records on a server of ours. They live on your phone and leave only as the record you share.

Rainbow’s End Checklists is an aid to preparation, not a substitute for the skipper’s judgment. Follow your training and the instructions of the Coast Guard.
