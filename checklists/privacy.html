Privacy Policy

Rainbow’s End Checklists — Pre-departure lists to Mayday — Effective October 9, 2026

The short version: Rainbow’s End Checklists sends nothing to us. Your boat, your lists and your records stay on your device, and leave it only when you share a record yourself. The phone’s position is read only to fill in the Mayday and Pan-Pan calls, and it is never stored or sent.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into Rainbow’s End Checklists is stored in the app’s own storage on your device. That is each boat’s profile (her name and description, how she is powered, her engines, length, registration, waters, what is aboard, and her call sign and MMSI if you enter them), your changes to the lists and the cards, your own lists, and every run and record, with the name you give as who completed it and any notes on skipped lines. The app has no server of ours and no account system.

Deleting a record, a boat or the app deletes this data from the device. If you back up your device, your backup may include the app’s data like any other app’s; backups are managed by Apple under Apple’s terms, and we never see them.

Position

The Mayday and Pan-Pan cards can fill in your position from the phone’s GPS. The app asks for your location while in use, through the system’s own prompt, only when you tap Use the GPS on one of those cards. Once you allow it, the card reads your position each time it opens and when you tap Read the position again, and shows it with its age.

The position is shown on the card and nowhere else. It is not stored, not written into any list or record, and not sent anywhere. The app never reads your location in the background. If you say no, the card works without it and tells you how to turn it on in Settings.

Records you share

A record is shared as a PDF or as text made on your device, handed to the system’s share sheet only when you tap Share as PDF or Share as text. Where it goes from there (Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files, a printer) is your choice, made each time. We never see it.

Links to our other apps

Some lines point to our other apps (Float Plan, Provisioning, Anchoring, Ship’s Purse, Logbook, Manifest). To open the app when you have it, Checklists asks the phone whether it is installed; the answer stays on the phone. When you don’t have it, the app shows that app’s page from Apple’s App Store inside Checklists.

Those App Store links carry a campaign tag naming the list the line is on (for example, “checklists-predeparture”). Apple uses the tag to count, in App Store Connect’s analytics, how many downloads came from each list. We see only those counts, never who tapped. The App Store page is Apple’s, under Apple’s terms. The Vessel Safety Check list’s last line opens the Coast Guard Auxiliary’s page, cgaux.org/vsc, in your browser.

The Lock Screen widget and the quick actions

The Lock Screen widget and the two actions on the app’s Home Screen icon only open the emergency cards. They read and show nothing of yours.

What the app does not do

Rainbow’s End Checklists contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party data-collection software. It has no background modes. It does not read your contacts, photos or anything else on your device. It works offline, because a boat is often out of signal; only the links to the App Store and to the Coast Guard Auxiliary’s page need a connection.

Purchases

Buying the app is handled entirely by the App Store. We receive no payment details, no card numbers, and no personal information from your purchase.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated first, with the effective date above revised, and the change described here.
