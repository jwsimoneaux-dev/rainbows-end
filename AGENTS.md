# rainbows-end — the public site

This repo is GitHub Pages for Rainbow's End Marine, LLC:
https://jwsimoneaux-dev.github.io/rainbows-end/ — the home page, the
privacy and support pages for Rainbow's End Anchoring at the root, and
one folder per companion app (monitor/, manifest/, floatplan/, galley/).
It is public; keep secrets and private links out of it.

## Rules

- **Nothing is authored here.** The canonical copy of every page lives in
  its app's repo under docs/ (Anchoring: set-and-hold/docs; the others in
  their own repos). Edit there, copy here, push both. The copies here may
  lag a canonical page on purpose until that app's release ships.
- Every page links favicon.png and apple-touch-icon.png at the root and
  closes with "Designed by Rainbow's End Marine. Apps for sailors, by
  sailors." The copyright line names Rainbow's End Marine, LLC.
- The store URLs (root privacy.html and support.html, the app folders) are
  registered in App Store Connect and must never move.
- **Contact and company site (Sept 18, 2026).** Email:
  support@RainbowsEndMarine.com (was rainbow@svrainbow.net). Company
  website: https://www.RainbowsEndMarine.com, a separate GoDaddy Airo site
  Jerry edits himself. Every footer here ends "· support@… ·
  www.RainbowsEndMarine.com". THIS site stays the home of the App Store
  URLs (privacy, support, marketing): the Airo site's privacy pages were
  generic and not accurate for these apps, and its App Store links were
  placeholders, when checked on Sept 18. JERRY'S DECISION (Sept 18): the Airo
