Regatta Suite

Three apps for the race and the trip home.

A volunteer crew, a race, the lay days and the delivery home: the watches and who stands them, the log of the whole trip, and word left ashore before the passage back. One purchase on the App Store, for less than the three bought separately.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad

The three apps

  • Rainbow’s End Watch BillPlans the crew’s watches, from the rolling two-hour stagger to the Swedish 6-6-4-4-4, checks them for gaps and short rest without ever calling a plan safe, tells the person on deck who to wake and when, and records each handover. Everyone aboard carries their own watches, with an alarm, in the free Watch Bill Crew. The plan travels as a code on your screen, so nothing needs a signal at sea.
  • Rainbow’s End LogbookOne logbook for the whole trip: the days, the places, up to six pictures a day, a position marked from the phone, and what it cost, shared as a PDF when you are home.
  • Rainbow’s End Float PlanBefore the delivery home, a PDF for someone reliable ashore: the boat, the people aboard, the itinerary, and the hour you are overdue, opening with a guide that tells them whom to call, in order.

One purchase

The Regatta Suite will be on the App Store at $8.99, against $12.97 for the three bought separately. Already own one or more? The App Store credits them, and you pay only for the rest. Its companions, the Owner’s Suite and the Charter Suite, are on the App Store now.

One free companion belongs to the crew and is not in the suite, because it costs nothing on its own: Watch Bill Crew, which puts each person’s watches and alarms on their own phone.

Offline, all three

No account, no server of ours, no tracking, in any of the three; nothing you enter leaves your device unless you share, print or export it yourself.

These apps are aids to seamanship, not a substitute for the skipper’s judgment.
