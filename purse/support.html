Getting started

What does Ship’s Purse do?

It keeps the books for a shared charter kitty. You set a budget, collect a share from each person, record every expense, count the envelope, and at the end the app gives each person one figure: what they hand the purse, or what the purse hands them.

Does everyone aboard need to buy it?

No. Only the bookkeeper needs Ship’s Purse. The rest of the crew can read the purse on their own phones with Ship’s Purse Guest, which is free.

Where is the sample?

On the Purses screen under Sample: a week in the Exumas with seven people, one at half share and one who joined on day four. It is read-only. Copy the sample as a new purse makes a copy you can change or delete.

Setting up

How do I set the budget?

Tap New purse. Enter the dates and the people, then fill in the worksheet: meals, fuel, moorings, dockage, water and ice, fees, the crew tip and a contingency. You set every rate. The total, divided by the shares, is the opening round per share.

How do meals work in the budget?

Count only the meals the purse pays for. Breakfasts, lunches and dinners aboard and meals ashore each have their own count and their own rate per person. One meal is one sitting for everyone aboard. Leave out any meal each person pays for themselves. When a dinner moves ashore, take it off the dinners aboard and add it to the meals ashore.

What is a share, and how do I set one below 1?

A share is how much of the purse a person is on the hook for. Everyone starts at 1. On the People screen, type the share: 0.5 for a child at half board, 0.57 for four days of seven.

Someone is joining mid-cruise.

Add them on the People screen when they arrive. They are not on the rounds already asked, so they owe only the rounds from then on, starting with the next top-up.

Can one purse use two currencies?

No. A purse has one currency, chosen when you create it.

During the cruise

How do I record money paid in?

Tap Transactions, then Paid in. Tap every name that paid. Each is filled in with what they owe on the rounds; change any amount that differs, pick how the money came in, and add them all at once.

Someone paid for fuel on their own card.

Enter it as an expense paid by that person, then choose how to square up. Reimburse means you hand them cash from the envelope and record it under Transactions > Reimburse. Offset means no cash moves: it counts against their next round, and anything left over comes back at settlement. Both end at the same figure.

The purse is running low.

Tap Transactions, then Top-up round, and enter the amount per share. Each person is asked for that amount times their share, less any credit they already have.

Our plans changed. How do I change the budget?

Tap Budget on the purse. The worksheet opens as you left it. Change the counts or rates and commit. With Refigure share due on, the difference becomes a round for everyone: a top-up if the budget went up, a reduction if it went down.

I entered something wrong.

Tap Strike… under the line in the ledger and give a reason. The line stays, crossed out, and every figure is worked out again as if it was never entered. Then enter it correctly.

How do I attach a receipt?

On an expense, tap Take a photo or Choose a photo. The photo is kept on your phone with the purse. Receipt › under the ledger line opens it.

What is the float?

The money paid in by Venmo, Zelle, card or anything other than cash, held in your own account for the purse. The envelope is the cash. Together they are what the purse holds.

The envelope count is off.

Tap Count and enter what the envelope holds. The app compares it with what the cash should be and records the difference. It changes no other figure. To explain the difference, enter an expense or a payment on purpose.

Can I rename a category?

Yes. On an expense, tap Rename or add categories…. A category that has entries can be renamed but not removed.

Settling

How does settling work?

Tap Settle. Whoever paid in less than their share due owes the purse the shortfall. Whoever paid in more, or paid for the boat beyond their share due, is owed the difference. What is left is shared back in proportion to each person’s share due. Each person gets one line. Mark each line received or paid as it happens; when all are marked, the purse holds nothing.

Why did someone get a few cents more?

Cents that do not divide evenly go to the bookkeeper’s line, and the Settle screen says so.

The purse spent more than everyone paid in.

Then there is nothing to share back. Each person’s part of the shortfall, in proportion to their share due, is added to what they owe the purse. A top-up round before settling does the same.

How do I close a purse?

Tap Close the purse. It moves to Past purses and becomes read-only. You can reopen it if a late receipt turns up.

Sharing with the crew

How does the crew see the purse?

Tap Share on the purse and switch sharing on. The app shows an eight-character code. Each person installs the free Ship’s Purse Guest and types the code. They see the ledger, the receipts, the settlement, and their own share due and balance. They cannot change anything.

Does the crew need an iCloud account?

No. Reading needs no account. Your phone, as the bookkeeper, must be signed into iCloud to share.

Who can read a shared purse?

Only someone with the code. The purse is sealed with a key made from the code before it leaves your phone, and nobody without the code can read it, including us.

The Share screen says Not yet shared.

Your phone has no signal, or iCloud has not answered yet. Every change is saved on your phone first and sent when there is signal: on the next change, when you open the app, and every minute while it is open. Try now sends it at once.

How do I stop sharing?

Switch sharing off on the Share screen. The purse and its receipt photos are taken down, and the code no longer opens anything. New code takes down the old code and shares under a new one.

Records and deleting

How do I get a copy for my records?

Tap Export. Share the statement makes a PDF with the settlement, spending by category and the full ledger. Share the spreadsheet makes a CSV for your books. Share the backup saves the whole purse as a file. Each goes to the share sheet.

How do I delete a purse?

On the Purses screen, swipe the purse to the left and tap Delete. The app asks first. The ledger and receipt photos go with it, and if the purse is shared it is taken down. It cannot be undone.

Does it work on the iPad?

Yes. In landscape the purse, the budget and the settlement show in two panes. In portrait, and on the phone, everything is one column with a summary pinned at the top.
