Rainbow’s End Handover

Take charge of the boat with confidence.

The base walks you round her in twenty minutes, hands you the keys, and by the time you notice the gouge in the topsides you are three anchorages away. Handover is the record of her condition when you take her over and when you hand her back. On your device, bought once, with no account.

In beta on TestFlight · headed for the App Store

What it does

  • Check-in, area by areaCabins, hull, deck, rig and sails, engine, systems, inventory lockers. Every photo is taken in the app and carries the phone’s time and position. Circle the damage on the photo itself.
  • Readings and equipmentFuel, water, battery, hour meters, holding tank; the equipment she is required to carry, ticked. At checkout every reading shows its difference.
  • The base’s briefingWrite down what the base told you, or record it as a voice memo once everyone recorded agrees.
  • Signed and sealedSign, and seal the check-in. Sealing makes the Condition at delivery report, with a fingerprint for each photo and for the walk.
  • Faults underwayRecord a fault when it happens: when it was reported and to whom, what repair was authorized, the receipts. Each says where it came from: found at check-in, arose underway, or found at checkout.
  • Checkout against the check-inThe camera shows each check-in photo faintly over the live view, so the picture you take now lines up with the one you took then.
  • ReturnsFuel, water, trash, linens, keys, documents, equipment, with the cost and the receipt for what you paid at the handback, checked against the readings.
  • The Final turnover reportEach area before and after, the difference in every reading, and every fault with its history, as a PDF through the share sheet.
  • Several photographersShare the areas out; each crew member photographs their own on the free Handover Crew app, and the photos come to your phone through iCloud, sealed under the handover’s code.
  • Works offlineNo account, no server of ours, nothing tracked. Only crew photographers need a signal, and their photos wait until there is one.

It came from our own charters, for the bareboat skipper. It serves the delivery captain, and an owner lending the boat to a friend, the same way.

What it won’t do

  • Take a walk-through photo from your library. Every picture’s time starts in the app’s own camera. Receipts are the one exception, and an imported one is marked as imported.
  • Claim more than a record. What the record is worth is for the parties to decide.
  • Keep the voyage or its costs. The days are Rainbow’s End Logbook; the kitty is Rainbow’s End Ship’s Purse.
  • Put your handover on a server of ours. It lives on your phone and leaves only as the report you send, or as crew photos sealed under your code.

Rainbow’s End Handover makes a record of the boat’s condition: each photo taken in the app, with the phone’s time and position, and a fingerprint that changes if a single byte changes. What that record is worth is for the parties, and if it comes to it a court, to decide.
