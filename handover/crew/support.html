Handover Crew — Support

Answers to the questions that come up. Anything not covered here: support@RainbowsEndMarine.com.

Getting started

What does Handover Crew do?

It lets you photograph your share of the boat at check-in and checkout, at the same time as the rest of the crew. Your photos go to the skipper’s Rainbow’s End Handover and land in the right areas, with your name. It is free.

How do I join?

On the skipper’s Handover, Crew photographers shows a code. Tap Scan the code and point the camera at it, or type the eight letters and numbers under Or type it. Then choose your name and tap Join as ….

Can I try it without a skipper?

Yes. On Join, tap Try the sample: a check-in walk for a sample boat, on this phone only. Nothing is sent.

Taking the photos

Which areas are mine?

The ones the skipper gave you. My areas lists them for the walk being taken, check-in or checkout. Tap one, then Take a photo.

What is the faint photo over the camera?

At checkout, the camera shows the check-in photo of the same area over the view, so your photo lines up with the one taken then. Choose Off, Faint or Half.

Why can’t I take a photo?

My areas says why: the skipper’s plan has not arrived yet (it comes when there is a signal), the skipper is not collecting photos right now, the walk is sealed, you have no area yet, or the skipper has stopped sharing. Ask the skipper.

Sending

What do Waiting, Sent and Arrived mean?

Waiting: on this phone, going when there is a signal. Sent: up, not yet on the skipper’s phone. Arrived: on the skipper’s Handover. Send now on Photos tries at once.

What if there is no signal?

Photos wait on your phone and go when there is a signal. The app checks when you open it, when you come back to it, and every 30 seconds while it is in front.

Do I need to be signed in to iCloud?

To send photos, yes: the phone’s own iCloud sign-in. Reading the skipper’s plan does not need it.

Can anyone else see my photos?

Anyone the skipper gives the handover’s code to. Your photos travel sealed under a key made from it, and we never see the code or open them. The privacy policy has the details.

Afterward

How do I clear my photos, or leave?

On Photos, Clear takes the ones that have arrived off this phone, and off iCloud if your phone has a signal. Leave this handover takes everything off this phone, and off iCloud if it has a signal; if any photo has not been sent, it says how many will be lost before it goes. Both ask twice.

Where is the report?

On the skipper’s phone. Handover Crew keeps no record of its own; the skipper’s Handover holds the record and makes the reports.
