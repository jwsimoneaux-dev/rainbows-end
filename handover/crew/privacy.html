Privacy Policy

Handover Crew — the free crew app for Rainbow’s End Handover — Effective September 26, 2026

The short version: Handover Crew sends nothing to us. Your photos go to the skipper who shared the handover, sealed through iCloud under the handover’s code, and nobody without the code can open them, including us.

What the app stores, and where

On your device: the handover you joined (its code, the boat’s name, the skipper’s plan of areas), the name you chose, and your photos until you clear them. The app has no server of ours and no account system. Leaving the handover, or deleting the app, removes them from the device.

The camera

The app asks for the camera, through the system’s own prompt, the first time you scan a code or take a photo. It reads the skipper’s code, and takes the photos of your areas. Photos are kept in the app’s own folder, never in your photo library, and each photo’s fingerprint is worked out on the device.

Position

Each photo records where it was taken, for the skipper’s record of the boat. The app asks for your location while in use, and reads it only while the camera is open. It never reads your location in the background.

What travels, and how

From your phone: your photos, each with its time, position and fingerprint, and the list of photos you have taken, under the name you chose. From the skipper’s phone to yours: the skipper’s plan (the boat, the areas and who has each), and at checkout the check-in photos of your areas, so your camera can line up with them.

Each record is sealed (AES-256-GCM) under a key made on the phone from the handover’s eight-character code, and stored in the public database of our iCloud container under a name made from the code. Without the code a record cannot be found or opened, by us or anyone else. We do not have the code. Sending uses your phone’s own iCloud sign-in; nothing is read from your own iCloud.

Clear on Photos takes the photos that have arrived off iCloud and off this phone. Leave this handover takes all of your records off iCloud and your photos off this phone.

What the app does not do

Handover Crew contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party data-collection software. It has no background modes. It does not read your contacts, your photo library or anything else on your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated first, with the effective date above revised, and the change described here.
