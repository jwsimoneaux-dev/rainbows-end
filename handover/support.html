Rainbow’s End Handover — Support

Answers to the questions that come up. Anything not covered here: support@RainbowsEndMarine.com.

Getting started

What does Handover do?

It makes a record of the boat’s condition when you take her over and when you hand her back. At check-in you walk her area by area with the app’s camera, mark any damage on the photos, take the readings, tick the equipment, note what the base told you, sign and seal. Underway it keeps each fault with its history. At checkout you walk her again against the check-in photos, and the app makes the Final turnover report. It works offline.

Where is the sample, and can I change it?

On the Handovers screen: Halcyon at Road Town, check-in sealed, with a windlass fault authorized. It shows the walk-through, the marked photos, the readings, the faults and both reports without using the camera. The sample itself is read-only; Copy the sample makes a copy of your own to try things on.

How do I start a handover?

Tap New handover. Enter the boat’s name, choose Monohull or Catamaran (the areas and the tanks follow from it), and fill in the charter company, the base, the skipper, the crew, the first day and the nights. The handover opens on Check-in.

Check-in

Why can I only use the app’s own camera?

So every photo’s time and position come from the phone at the moment it was taken, never from a picture made earlier. Receipts are the one exception: they may come from your photo library, because a receipt often arrives by email, and an imported one is marked as imported.

How do I mark damage on a photo?

Open the photo and tap where the damage is. A numbered circle appears; choose its size. Tap Record a fault here to turn the marks into a fault with its own history, or tie a mark to a fault already recorded. Remove this mark takes it off.

What is the fingerprint?

A SHA-256 fingerprint of the photo’s bytes, worked out on the phone. If a single byte of the photo changes, the fingerprint changes. Each photo’s fingerprint is printed in the report, and sealing a walk fingerprints the whole walk. Check the photo files on Reports works each one out again and says whether they still match.

Can I record the base’s briefing?

Yes, as notes, a voice memo, or both. The app asks first: turn on Everyone recorded agrees before Record the briefing will start. The recording stays on the phone.

What does sealing do?

Signers sign the fingerprint they see, and sealing stores the time and the fingerprint of the whole walk. After that the check-in is fixed. If anything changes after someone has signed, their signature goes stale and the walk cannot be sealed until they sign again. Anything written in after the seal is kept, and marked Added after sealing in the report.

Underway

How do I record a fault?

On Faults, tap Record a fault, or Record a fault here on a marked photo. Say what it is and take a photo of it. Then add its history as it happens: when you reported it and to whom, what repair was authorized, when it was repaired, the receipts, and when it was closed. Each fault says where it came from: found at check-in, arose underway, or found at checkout.

Can I still add to a fault after the check-in is sealed?

Yes. A fault’s history can always be added to, even after checkout. The sealed parts themselves do not change.

Checkout

What is the faint photo over the camera?

At checkout the camera shows the check-in photo of the same area over the live view, so the photo you take now lines up with the one you took then. Choose Off, Faint or Half.

Where do I enter the fuel and water I paid for at the handback?

On Returns. Turn on Refueled or Water tanks filled, then enter the amount, how much went in, and the receipt. If the fuel reading at checkout is not full while Refueled is on, Returns says so.

Can I record fuel I bought in the middle of the week?

No, on purpose. Handover records the boat’s condition, and the costs of the handback. Costs during the voyage belong in Rainbow’s End Logbook or Rainbow’s End Ship’s Purse.

How do I share the reports?

On Reports, share the Condition at delivery report after check-in and the Final turnover report after checkout. Each is a PDF, handed to the phone’s share sheet, so you choose where it goes: Mail, AirDrop, Messages, Files or a printer.

Crew photographers

Can several of us photograph at once?

Yes, with the free Handover Crew app on each crew phone. On Crew photographers, add the crew, Share the areas out, and Start sharing. Each crew member scans the code, chooses their name, and photographs only their own areas. Their photos come to your phone and land in the right areas with their names.

What if there is no signal?

Photos wait on the crew phone and go when there is a signal. Your phone checks every 45 seconds while it is open, or tap Check for photos now. Sending from a crew phone needs its iCloud sign-in.

Can anyone else see the crew’s photos?

No. They travel through iCloud sealed under a key made from the handover’s code, and without the code nobody can find or open them, including us. Stop sharing takes down what your phone put up. The privacy policy has the details.

What it is and is not

What is the report worth if there is a dispute over the deposit?

It is a record. Each photo was taken in the app, its time and position come from the phone, and each photo and sealed report carries a fingerprint. What that record is worth is for the parties to decide.

Does it work on the iPad?

Yes. In landscape every main screen shows two panes side by side. In portrait, and on the phone, it is one column.

How do I delete a handover?

Open it, go to Details, and tap Delete this handover. Its photos, recordings and signatures go with it. It cannot be undone.
