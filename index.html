Rainbow’s End

May you find a rainbow’s end wherever you sail or set your anchor.

An anchor & rode calculator for iPhone. It works out how much rode to pay out for the conditions you are in, recommends tackle from the gear you actually carry, and says plainly what to watch.

  • Support & FAQSetup, how the numbers work, and how to report one that looks wrong.
  • Privacy PolicyShort, because the app collects nothing.
