What it won’t do

  • It won’t tell you whether she is safe offshore. No ratio can.
  • It won’t estimate displacement. Enter the published figure.
  • It won’t size a planing powerboat’s speed.
  • It won’t send anything anywhere. Your figures stay in this browser.

The numbers describe a design and a load. The sea decides the rest. Rainbow’s End Boat Calculator is an aid to judgment, not a substitute for it.
