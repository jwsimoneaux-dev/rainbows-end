Rainbow's End Float Plan

Leave word ashore before you leave the dock. A float plan tells someone reliable exactly what to say to rescuers if you don't come home. This app fills one out with you and hands you a PDF made to be emailed.

What it does

  • Grouped the way a sailor thinks — vessel, communication, safety & survival, the people aboard, the itinerary, and your shore contacts, each section with a line on why searchers want it.
  • Remembers your boat — fill her particulars once; next trip you change only the itinerary. "New trip" keeps the boat, gear, people, and contacts and clears the voyage.
  • A PDF made to be emailed — one tap builds the plan and opens the share sheet; Mail is two taps from a filled plan.
  • A guide for the holder — the PDF opens with "If we are overdue": whom to call, in what order, with your actual contacts, phone numbers, and check-in deadline already filled in. The person holding your plan never has to hunt.
  • The whole inventory — modeled on the official USCG/CGAUX float plan, from hull colors and MMSI to EPIRB and PLB numbers, tow vehicle, and the reason for every stop.

A float plan is never filed with the Coast Guard — it goes to someone reliable ashore. Tell them if plans change, and close the plan when everyone is home safe.

Now in beta

Rainbow's End Float Plan is on TestFlight, headed for the App Store. It's fully offline — no account, no server; the plan leaves your device only when you send it.
