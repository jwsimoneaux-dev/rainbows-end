Rainbow’s End Float Plan

Boating safety, sent ashore.

Leave word ashore before you leave the dock. A float plan tells someone reliable exactly what to say to rescuers if you don't come home. This app fills one out with you and hands you a PDF made to be emailed.

In beta on TestFlight · headed for the App Store

What it does

  • Grouped the way a sailor thinksvessel, communication, safety & survival, the people aboard, the itinerary, and your shore contacts, each section with a line on why searchers want it.
  • Remembers your boatsfill a boat's particulars once and she's saved; keep several and switch between them with a tap. The boat, her gear, the skipper, and the tow vehicle fill themselves; the crew, itinerary, and contacts are yours to write each voyage.
  • A PDF made to be emailedone tap builds the plan and opens the share sheet; Mail is two taps from a filled plan.
  • A guide for the holderthe PDF opens with "If we are overdue": whom to call, in what order, with your actual contacts, phone numbers, and check-in deadline already filled in. The person holding your plan never has to hunt.
  • The whole inventorymodeled on the official USCG/CGAUX float plan, from hull colors and MMSI to EPIRB and PLB numbers, tow vehicle, and the reason for every stop.

A float plan is never filed with the Coast Guard — it goes to someone reliable ashore. Tell them if plans change, and close the plan when everyone is home safe.

Now in beta

Rainbow's End Float Plan is on TestFlight, headed for the App Store. It's fully offline — no account, no server; the plan leaves your device only when you send it.
