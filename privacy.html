Privacy Policy

Rainbow's End — Anchor & Rode Calculator · Effective September 2, 2026

The short version: Rainbow's End sends nothing anywhere. Everything you enter — and, if you use the anchor watch, your GPS position — stays on your device. The one exception is yours to switch on: the remote watch, which copies the watch to your own iCloud so your other devices can monitor it, and to nobody else.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into Rainbow's End — boat particulars, anchors and rodes, saved boats, unit preferences, and the conditions you enter — is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted anywhere: the app has no server and no account system, and it makes no network requests of its own. The single optional exception is the remote watch described below, which carries the anchor watch to your own iCloud and nothing else.

Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your device, your backup may include the app's data like any other app's; those backups are managed by Apple or Google under their own terms, and we never see them.

Location — the anchor watch

Since version 1.2.0 the app includes an optional anchor watch: it draws a fence around your anchor and sounds an alarm if the boat's GPS position leaves it. If you use this feature, the app asks for location permission ("while using the app" only) and reads your position from the device's GPS.

Your position is used on the device and only on the device: it is compared against the fence, shown to you on screen, and the fence itself is kept in the app's local storage so the watch survives an app restart. The trail on the Watch tab is kept only in the app's memory while the watch is armed. No position is transmitted or shared — GPS works from satellites alone — unless you switch on the remote watch described next, which copies it to your own iCloud and nowhere else. Disarming the watch stops all use of location, and you can revoke the permission any time in your device settings. If you never use the anchor watch, the app never asks for or touches your location.

iCloud — the remote watch

Since version 1.3.0 an armed anchor watch can be shared with your other devices. This is off unless you turn on "Share to my other devices" on the Watch tab, and it needs an iPhone or iPad signed into iCloud. When it is on, the boat device writes the state of the watch to the private part of your own iCloud account, and any other device signed into the same Apple ID can read it back and show the watch, with an alarm ashore if the boat breaks out of her fence or falls silent.

What is written is what the Watch tab shows, and nothing more: the fence and the way you anchored, when the watch was armed, the boat's current position and GPS accuracy, how far she has ranged, whether she is inside or outside the fence, the time of the report, and a thinned recent trail of at most 200 points. It is one small record, overwritten in place about every 20 seconds while sharing is on, so no history accumulates. Your boat particulars, gear, and saved boats are never included.

That record is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and Apple's iCloud terms, in your private iCloud database: the app has no server of its own, we cannot read the record, and no other iCloud user can. Turning sharing off, or disarming the watch, stops the writing and deletes the record from your iCloud, so nothing lingers between watches. If the boat device has no signal at that moment the delete cannot go through, and the last record stays until the next shared watch replaces it; you can also remove it yourself, with any other app data, from your device's iCloud storage settings. If you never turn sharing on, the app never touches iCloud.

What the app does not do

Rainbow's End contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party data-collection software. It does not read your contacts, photos, or anything else on your device. It works offline — by design, because it is meant to be used at anchor, where there is often no signal. The remote watch is the one feature that needs a connection, and it is optional.

Purchases

Buying the app is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details, no card numbers, and no personal information from your purchase.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.
