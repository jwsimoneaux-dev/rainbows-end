Privacy Policy

Rainbow's End Manifest — Boat Safety Checklist, Briefing & Log · Effective August 30, 2026

The short version: Rainbow's End Manifest sends nothing anywhere. Your boat's papers, your gear list, and your cruising log stay on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into Rainbow's End Manifest — your boat's particulars and papers (including, if you enter them, the owner's name and address, hull ID, registration numbers, radio call sign and MMSI), your safety-equipment checklist and where each item is stowed, your maintenance dates, fuel fills, and every entry in your cruising log — is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere to transmit it: the app has no server, no account system, and makes no network requests.

The owner details deserve a plain word of their own: you enter them because a vessel's manifest traditionally records them, and they live in the same local storage as everything else. We never see them. Nobody sees them but you.

Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your device, your backup may include the app's data like any other app's; those backups are managed by Apple or Google under their own terms, and we never see them.

What the app does not do

Rainbow's End Manifest contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party data-collection software. It does not use your location. It does not read your contacts, photos, or anything else on your device. It works entirely offline — by design, because it is meant to be used aboard, where there is often no signal.

Purchases

Buying the app is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details, no card numbers, and no personal information from your purchase.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above — for example, by adding an online feature — this policy will be updated first, with the effective date above revised, and the change described plainly rather than buried.

Contact

Questions about this policy: rainbow@svrainbow.net.

Rainbow's End Manifest is an aid to preparation, not a substitute for the regulations or for a Coast Guard Auxiliary Vessel Safety Check. May you find a rainbow's end and treasure found wherever you sail or set your anchor.
