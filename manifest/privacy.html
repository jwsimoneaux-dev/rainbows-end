Privacy Policy

Rainbow's End Manifest — Boat Safety Checklist, Briefing & Log · Effective September 2, 2026

The short version: Rainbow's End Manifest sends nothing to us — ever. Your boat's papers, your gear list, and your cruising log stay on your device, unless you switch on iCloud sync, in which case they travel only through your own iCloud, which we cannot read.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into Rainbow's End Manifest — your boat's particulars and papers (including, if you enter them, the owner's name and address, hull ID, registration numbers, radio call sign and MMSI), your safety-equipment checklist and where each item is stowed, your maintenance dates, fuel fills, and every entry in your cruising log — is stored in the app's local storage on your own device. The app has no server of ours and no account system, and with iCloud sync switched off (its factory setting) it makes no network requests at all.

The owner details deserve a plain word of their own: you enter them because a vessel's manifest traditionally records them, and they live in the same local storage as everything else. We never see them. Nobody sees them but you.

Deleting the app deletes this data from the device. If iCloud sync was on, the copy in your own iCloud remains until you remove it (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage) — which also means reinstalling with sync on brings your book back. If you back up your device, your backup may include the app's data like any other app's; backups and iCloud alike are managed by Apple under Apple's terms, and we never see either.

iCloud sync — your own cloud, not ours

Edit boat offers one switch: Sync her books across your devices. It is off until you turn it on, on each device. Switched on, the app keeps a copy of your fleet — every boat's manifest, gear, dates, and log — in the private database of your own iCloud account, so an iPhone and an iPad signed into the same Apple ID show the same book. That record is Apple's infrastructure under Apple's terms, readable only by your Apple ID: we have no access to it, no copy of it, and no way to know it exists. Nothing about this creates an account with us — there is still no us to have an account with. Switch sync off and the app goes back to making no network requests at all; delete the app's data from iCloud at any time in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

What the app does not do

Rainbow's End Manifest contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party data-collection software. It does not use your location. It does not read your contacts, photos, or anything else on your device. It works offline — by design, because it is meant to be used aboard, where there is often no signal; iCloud sync, if you turn it on, simply catches up the next time the device finds a connection.

Purchases

Buying the app is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details, no card numbers, and no personal information from your purchase.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above — for example, by adding an online feature — this policy will be updated first, with the effective date above revised, and the change described plainly rather than buried.
