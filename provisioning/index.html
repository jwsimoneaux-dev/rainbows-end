Rainbow’s End Provisioning

Eight crew, seven days, one galley and one trip to the store.

Plan every meal of the voyage on a calendar, mark what’s eaten ashore, and the calendar becomes the grocery list.

On the App Store for iPhone and iPad

What it does

  • VoyageDates, crew and a provisioning pattern: full (every meal aboard) or split (breakfasts and lunches aboard, most dinners ashore, the way charter weeks run). Say who drinks what, which food allergies are aboard, and whether the crew drinks from the tanks.
  • CalendarBreakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for each day, each aboard, ashore or off, at anchor or underway. Suggestions plan fresh meals early and one-pot meals underway. It warns about food that won’t keep to its day, meals with a crew allergen, and water the tanks can’t cover.
  • GalleyMore than forty meals with per-person amounts and cooking steps for a two-burner galley. Add your own, and record what’s already aboard.
  • ListOne grocery list: each meal’s amounts times the crew aboard that day, less what’s aboard, rounded up to real packs. Adjust it, split it by aisle among several shoppers and send each their share, check items into the cart, print it and the calendar as PDFs, or export spreadsheets.

Why it’s different

  • Arithmetic you can checkYou can’t buy 13 eggs, so it buys 18, and the spreadsheet shows needed, already aboard and to buy.
  • Built for a boatKeeping windows, the ship’s tanks, meals underway, dinners ashore and the charter pattern.
  • OfflineNo account, no server, no tracking. Nothing leaves your device unless you print, export or send it, or switch on iCloud sync to keep your iPhone and iPad the same through your own iCloud.

We built it aboard our ketch Rainbow, from the provisioning lists of our own charter weeks.

What it won’t do

  • It doesn’t order groceries or connect to a store.
  • It isn’t a recipe app: no photos, no scaling.
  • It doesn’t plan emergency water or safety stores.

Keeping windows and water rates are estimates from cruising practice. An aid to judgment, not a substitute for it.
